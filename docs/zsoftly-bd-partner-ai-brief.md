# ZSoftly BD Partner AI Brief

Use this file as the single source of truth for a business development partner
or their AI assistant when evaluating projects for ZSoftly delivery.

## Purpose

The business development partner's role is to find, qualify, and pursue
projects. ZSoftly Technologies Inc. o/a ZSoftly is the backend technical
delivery team.

When given a project, opportunity, RFP, client need, job post, or lead, the AI
should answer:

1. Can ZSoftly credibly deliver this?
2. Should the BD partner pursue it?
3. If yes, how should the opportunity be targeted, positioned, and qualified?
4. If no, why not?
5. If it needs a partner, what part should ZSoftly deliver and what part needs
   another specialist?

## Company Positioning

ZSoftly is a cloud, DevOps, security, AI, and professional services company
serving Canada, the United States, and the United Kingdom as primary markets.

ZSoftly builds and operates ZSoftly Cloud Platform (ZCP), a public cloud
platform product and sovereign/private cloud offering focused on
jurisdiction-aware data residency, regional control, open-source
infrastructure, predictable operations, and reduced hyperscaler lock-in.

ZSoftly also delivers customer IT projects through its Professional Services
business unit, including cloud and platform builds, managed services,
full-stack development, AI agent delivery, web/application development,
maintenance, modernization, hosting, and operational support.

The BD partner may sell or originate the opportunity, but ZSoftly should be
positioned as the delivery organization for technical architecture,
implementation, management, and ongoing support.

## Primary Markets

Prioritize opportunities in:

- Canada
- United States
- United Kingdom

Do not describe ZSoftly as Canada-only. Sovereign cloud should mean the right
jurisdiction, controls, residency, privacy, and operational model for the buyer.
For a Canadian buyer, emphasize Canadian data residency where relevant. For a US
or UK buyer, align sovereignty and residency to their required jurisdiction.

## Best-Fit Buyers

Prioritize buyers that need senior technical delivery, not commodity staffing:

- Public sector, municipalities, government agencies, Crown or public bodies,
  education, healthcare, Indigenous organizations, legal, financial services,
  and critical infrastructure.
- SaaS, fintech, healthcare technology, cybersecurity, data-intensive, and
  regulated companies with production workloads.
- Organizations with cloud, Kubernetes, DevOps, security, data, AI, application,
  or infrastructure pain.
- Buyers with clear urgency, budget, decision-maker involvement, and production
  impact.
- Buyers that value privacy, governance, regional control, accountable
  engineering, managed services, and long-term support.

## Opportunity Decision Rules

Use these decisions:

- `PURSUE`: Strong fit. The BD partner should pursue and position ZSoftly as
  the delivery team.
- `PURSUE WITH PARTNER`: Good opportunity, but another specialist is needed for
  SOC, hardware, HPC, research computing, niche compliance, or other proof gaps.
- `MORE INFO`: Potential fit, but there are missing facts that materially
  affect eligibility, scope, budget, or delivery.
- `DO NOT PURSUE`: Weak fit, poor buyer quality, missed mandatory deadline, or
  not credible for ZSoftly.

## Scoring Guide

Score each opportunity from 1 to 10:

- `9-10`: Direct ZSoftly fit, strong buyer, clear budget, strong delivery
  proof. Pursue aggressively.
- `7-8`: Good fit. Pursue if deadlines and requirements are manageable.
- `5-6`: Possible fit, but needs clarification or a partner.
- `3-4`: Weak fit or high delivery/proof risk. Usually do not pursue.
- `1-2`: Not aligned. Do not pursue.

## Mandatory Gate Checks

Before recommending pursuit, check:

- Has the close date passed?
- Has the question deadline passed?
- Has an intent-to-respond deadline passed?
- Is there a mandatory site visit, NDA, registration, portal account, or
  prequalification step?
- Are there mandatory forms, pricing sheets, reference forms, insurance,
  certifications, security clearances, or financial requirements?
- Does the buyer require named resources?
- Does the buyer require local presence, residency, or clearances in a specific
  jurisdiction?
- Does the opportunity require proof ZSoftly does not have?

If a mandatory gate is missed, recommend `DO NOT PURSUE` unless the buyer
explicitly allows late registration or another compliant route.

## How to Target Good Opportunities

When the decision is `PURSUE`, the BD partner should target the buyer with this
positioning:

1. Lead with the buyer's specific problem, not generic company language.
2. Position ZSoftly as the technical delivery team for cloud, DevOps, security,
   AI, platform, application, or managed-service execution.
3. Emphasize production ownership, accountable engineering, and ongoing support.
4. For sovereign or regulated work, emphasize jurisdiction-aware data
   residency, regional control, privacy, governance, and open-source
   infrastructure.
5. For cloud/platform work, mention ZCP, private cloud, CloudStack, OpenStack,
   Proxmox, Ceph, Kubernetes, networking, storage, backup, observability, and
   managed operations where relevant.
6. For AI work, mention secure AI infrastructure, private AI hosting, RAG,
   workflow automation, and AI agent design/build/manage/maintain.
7. For web/app work, mention full-stack development, solutions architecture,
   hosting, security hardening, integrations, modernization, and ongoing
   support.
8. Ask practical qualification questions about timeline, budget, current
   environment, required compliance, decision process, and support expectations.

## Claims to Avoid

Do not claim that ZSoftly has:

- Mature 24/7 SOC/MDR operations unless a SOC partner is included.
- Specialized HPC research computing references unless confirmed.
- OEM hardware supply-chain capability unless confirmed.
- Required certifications, clearances, insurance, or partner statuses unless
  confirmed.
- Unlimited staffing capacity.
- Guaranteed compliance before reviewing the buyer's mandatory requirements.

Use careful language such as:

- "ZSoftly can deliver the cloud/platform/application layer."
- "Named resources will be confirmed based on scope, clearance, residency, and
  availability."
- "A specialist partner may be added for SOC, HPC, hardware, or niche
  compliance requirements."
- "Final architecture and controls will be scoped against the buyer's mandatory
  requirements."

## Final Decision Rule

Pursue when ZSoftly can credibly be the delivery team for work involving cloud,
platform engineering, DevOps, managed services, security, jurisdiction-aware
data residency, AI infrastructure, AI agents, full-stack applications, websites,
or meaningful IT project delivery.

Do not pursue when the opportunity is generic, low-value, unclear, price-only,
or requires proof ZSoftly cannot credibly provide alone.
